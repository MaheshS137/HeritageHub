About HeritageHub

Discover Karnataka. Preserve its Heritage.

HeritageHub is a digital archive dedicated to documenting Karnataka's monuments, festivals, traditional arts, folk dances, cuisine, handicrafts, and customs using real GeoJSON district boundaries and source-verified records across all 31 districts.

Project Objectives

Connect authentic district boundaries with structured JSON heritage records backed by public institutional sources.

Information Sources

UNESCO World Heritage Centre, IP India GI Registry, Sangeet Natak Akademi, KSTDC, Karnataka Forest Department, and geoBoundaries IND-ADM2 (ODbL 1.0).

Future Scope

Expand full 7-pillar monographs across all 31 districts and integrate post-2021 standalone Vijayanagara GIS boundary polygons once openly licensed.
